# RizzCoach - Usage Disclaimer & Safety Policy

**RizzCoach is an open-source educational and communication coaching assistant.**

### 1. Authentic Communication
The goal of this software is to assist users in building genuine confidence, understanding conversational dynamics, and improving their social communication. It is **not** intended for harassment, spam, impersonation, deception, or non-consensual behavior.

### 2. Third-Party Platform Terms of Service
Users are solely responsible for ensuring their use of adapters, webhooks, or automation complies with the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines of any third-party platforms (e.g., Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, Meta/Instagram, Twilio, Telegram). The maintainers do not encourage unauthorized scraping or automated bot actions that violate third-party terms.

### 3. Data Privacy & Zero Telemetry
RizzCoach is designed to be 100% self-hosted and private. It contains **no telemetry, phone home mechanisms, or central data collection**. When you run RizzCoach with local models (e.g., Ollama or DeepSeek), your private conversations, contacts, and personal photos never leave your local machine.

### 4. No Professional Guarantee
The software does not provide legal, psychological, or medical counseling. Users should use sound personal judgment in all interpersonal interactions.
